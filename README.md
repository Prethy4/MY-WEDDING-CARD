# Bespoke Royal Wedding Invitation Card 💍✨

An ultra-aesthetic, realistic luxury 3D wedding invitation crafted with **Pure HTML, Vanilla CSS, and JavaScript**. Designed as a genuine physical keepsake stationery card rather than a typical website.

---

## 🎨 Color Palette & Aesthetic Design
- **Almond Gold**: Rich shimmering metallic foil gradients, embossed borders, royal calligraphy, and satin ribbon belly band.
- **Emerald Red (Royal Velvet)**: Deep ruby-crimson velvet gatefold cardstock with authentic paper grain and split center medallion.
- **Soft Blush Pink**: Delicate blooming peonies and watercolor floral bouquets with dynamic moving petals and golden foliage.

---

## 🌟 Key Features

1. **Realistic 3D Gatefold Card Construction**:
   - Closed state features a royal Emerald Red velvet jacket with an Almond Gold split center medallion and corner filigrees.
   - Tied with an Almond Gold satin ribbon belly band displaying bride & groom names and date.
   - Sealed with an authentic 3D Emerald Red & Gold Wax Seal.
   - Interactive 3D mouse parallax tilt that catches light and shifts shadows realistically.

2. **Dynamic Opening Experience & Moving Flowers**:
   - Touching the wax seal triggers an explosion of golden sparkles.
   - The belly band smoothly dissolves and the two gatefold doors swing outward in genuine 3D perspective (`transform: rotateY(...)`).
   - Four ornate corner floral bouquets and side peonies bloom and expand dynamically with organic floating sway animations.
   - Fullscreen Canvas Petal Shower: Soft blush pink peony petals, ruby rose petals, and shimmering gold dust particles drift and tumble continuously across the screen.

3. **Exquisite Stationery Typography & Interior Details**:
   - Heavy almond silk parchment texture with gold foil framing.
   - Shimmering gold calligraphy couple names (*PRE THY & DONNO*).
   - Auspicious Date cartouche, venue details, and ceremony schedule timeline.
   - Foil shimmer light sweep effect across the text upon opening.

4. **Live Personalization System**:
   - Click the **"Personalize"** button in the top toolbar to instantly update Bride & Groom names, date, time, venue, and blessing inscription in real time without touching code.

5. **Expanded Botanical Rose Petal Palette**:
   - Rich multi-hue spectrum: Velvet Ruby Red, Royal Crimson, Peony Blush Pink, Peach Champagne, Deep Burgundy Wine, and radiant Almond Gold flakes.
   - Interactive mouse wind draft: moving the cursor gently sways the falling petals.

6. **Interactive RSVP Modal**:
   - Click "RSVP With Honor" inside the card to open an elegant wax-sealed guest response card with attendance selection and celebratory wishes.

7. **Re-seal & Replay**:
   - One-click "Re-seal Card" button to close the card and replay the opening animation anytime.

---

## 🚀 How to Run

Simply open `index.html` in any modern web browser (Google Chrome, Edge, Safari, Firefox), or run a lightweight local server:

```powershell
python -m http.server 8080
```
Then visit: `http://localhost:8080` in your browser.
